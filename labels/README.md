# Dev labels

Our own annotations of the sample videos, in the `ground_truth.json` format of the starter kit
(one file per video; `scripts/merge_labels.py` joins them).

- **C3902**: Dev labels for C3902 (team review of candidate strips at loose thresholds + contact sheets every 20 s). Boundaries follow the task's start/end conventions to within ~0.5 s. v0: jaywalking and failure_to_yield reviewed candidate by candidate; red_light/stop_line checked against every stop-line crossing (all on green/amber); stopped_vehicle = vehicles parked at the far curb all clip long.

Caveat: candidates were generated by our own rules at loose thresholds, so recall measured on
these labels is optimistic; events no rule looks for could be missing.

- **C3896, C3897, C3905** (midday, midday, dusk): every detection of our rules was reviewed on image
  tiles (mid-event frame with the involved road users) and accepted or rejected; wrong-way hits inside
  the junction and one failure_to_yield with the pedestrian on the far sidewalk were rejected. Boundaries
  are the detected ones. These labels therefore measure **precision only**; recall is not measured.
