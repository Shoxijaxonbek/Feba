RoadSense

A fixed 4K traffic camera in Tashkent, turned into a time-stamped list of traffic violations and a frame-by-frame accident-risk score.

Detection and tracking give us every road user. The traffic light's own state and a hand-drawn map of the junction tell us what they are allowed to do.

Problem & approach

A little learning and a lot of geometry

Part A: turn an .mp4 into traffic events [start_sec, end_sec, label] across 14 official classes, scored by macro-F1 over temporal-IoU thresholds 0.3, 0.5 and 0.7. Part B: a causal per-frame probability that an accident starts within 5 seconds.

Models and data

Detector
YOLO11m, open COCO weights (person, bicycle, car, motorcycle, bus, truck). The medium size is the accuracy/speed sweet spot; see the ablations under Metrics.
Tracker
ByteTrack in three groups (vehicles, two-wheelers, pedestrians), so a cyclist never swaps identity with a pedestrian.
Decoding
PyAV decodes only the I- and P-frames of the camera's I-B-B-P GOP: every third frame, ≈10 fps, at about 5× realtime.
Data
The organisers' sample videos only. The scene layout is drawn once on this fixed view; the lane directions are learned from our own trajectories; our dev set is our own labels of the samples.
Runtime
Part A runs faster than the video plays, far inside the 3× budget. Exact numbers are under Metrics.

Traffic-light reader

Small boxes around each lamp of the main signal head are checked for a red, amber or green glow. The reader recovers the fixed 80 s cycle: red 37 s, red + amber 3 s, green 33 s, flashing green 4 s, amber 3 s.

Live demo

Run it on your own clip

Upload a short .mp4. The server runs the full pipeline and returns the same views as the results above.

The demo server has no GPU, so it uses a lighter setting than our submission: YOLO11s at 960 px and 5 fps instead of YOLO11m at 1280 px and 10 fps. On our dev labels that costs accuracy (Score A 0.54 vs 0.84; small, distant road users and the U-turn are missed), but a 2-minute clip comes back in about 3 minutes.
